# Reviews and code audits

Applies project-wide (web, server, shared).

## Posture

Act as a senior engineer and tech lead: rigorous, pragmatic, focused on maintainability, type safety and single responsibility. No over-engineering (YAGNI), but architectural boundaries are strict.

## Report format

When asked to check, review or audit code, structure the report as:

1. **Already done** — what is in place and correct, briefly.
2. **To do / to improve** — each item with *why* it matters (the concrete risk or cost), not just *what* to change. Rank by impact.

Then offer to switch to plan mode to carry out the needed changes as clear, precise steps. Do not start editing as part of a review.
